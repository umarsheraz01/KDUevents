Please read these guidelines before you submit an event proposal or book a campus venue.

Event Guidelines & Rules

These rules apply to every student, society, and department event hosted through KDU Events.

Important: An event is confirmed only after the KDU Events admin team approves it by email. Do not advertise or sell tickets until you have received approval.

1 Planning & Approval

  • Apply early. Submit your proposal at least 14 days before the event (21 days for events with 150+ guests).
  • Name a lead organizer. Every event needs one responsible student or staff member with a valid KDU student or staff ID.
  • Give complete details. Include the date, time, venue, expected attendance, and any equipment you need in the application.
  • Changes need approval. If you change the date, venue, or size of an approved event, tell the admin team at least 5 days in advance.

2 Venue Use

  • Stay within capacity. Never exceed the posted seating limit of the room or hall.
  • Keep to your time slot. Setup and cleanup must fit inside your booked hours. Campus events end by 10:00 PM unless approved otherwise.
  • Respect the space. Do not attach tape, nails, or stickers to walls. Use the provided stands for banners and posters.
  • Leave it clean. Return furniture to its original layout and remove all rubbish and decorations after the event.

3 Safety & Conduct

  • Keep exits clear. Fire exits, corridors, and emergency equipment must never be blocked.
  • No alcohol or smoking at student events on campus unless the university gives special permission in writing.
  • Be inclusive. Events must be respectful and welcoming to all students, whatever their nationality, religion, or background.
  • Report incidents. Report any injury, damage, or safety problem to campus security and the admin team straight away.

4 Food, Promotion & Equipment

  • Food and drinks are allowed only in approved venues. Request catering in your proposal.
  • Promotion may begin only after approval. Posters go on official notice boards only.
  • AV equipment such as projectors, microphones, and speakers must be requested in advance and returned in good condition.
  • Photography: tell guests if the event will be photographed or recorded, and respect anyone who asks not to be filmed.

5 Cancellations & Penalties

  • Cancel early. Cancel at least 48 hours before the event by emailing admin@kduevents.co.kr so the venue can be freed for others.
  • No-shows and repeated late cancellations may stop a society from booking venues for the rest of the semester.
  • Damage to university property is the responsibility of the organizing society or person.
